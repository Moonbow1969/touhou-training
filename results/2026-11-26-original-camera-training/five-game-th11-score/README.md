# Does TH11's original score help a five-game policy learn?

The 16 TH11 training credits each contained exactly three lost lives and zero gained lives. Their undiscounted stock returns were all −3. The first and final loss occurred at different times and the original score at the last hit ranged from 37,680 to 4,088,500. The stock-only objective can still favor delaying a loss through discounting. It ignores scoring events between losses. [Per-credit stock events](../th11-original-ppo/verified-stock-reward.json)

Two five-title PPO fits share their SFT initialization, observations, actions, 48 original trajectories, optimizer seed and four epochs. The experimental fit adds a bounded score reward on TH11. If the original points increase from P to Q over a policy action, its bonus is 0.30 × (F(Q) − F(P)), where F(P) = P / (P + 233,975). The scale is the median final-loss score of the training credits. The entire additional return per game is less than 0.30. A life loss costs 1. The other four titles keep their original rewards. Scoring events supplied a positive increment on 1,728 of the 3,765 TH11 decisions. [Reward audit](score-reward-audit.json) · [Fitted checkpoint and optimizer history](policy/report.json)

The original evaluation compares the two five-game checkpoints on 16 fresh TH11 controller seeds. Each difficulty has eight pairs. The primary endpoint is the original Stage 2 transition. Original score at the first and final losses, spare lives, Power and effective casts describe how the policies played. [Frozen evaluation protocol](camera-protocol.json)

All 32 original evaluation credits finished. Each policy reached Stage 2 on 0/8 Hard and 0/8 Lunatic starts and exhausted its spare lives. Mean settled score changed from 581,593 to 448,016 on Hard, a paired difference of −133,576 (95% bootstrap interval −695,333 to +566,366). On Lunatic it changed from 865,738 to 988,538, a difference of +122,800 (−551,475 to +776,866). Effective casts per credit went from 1.00 to 0.50 on Hard and stayed at 0.625 on Lunatic. Both policies ended with zero Power. The score bonus supplied a denser training signal and yielded no demonstrated gain in reaching Stage 2 or scoring.
