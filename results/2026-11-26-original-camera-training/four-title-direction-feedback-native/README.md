# From replay direction prediction to original-game survival

TH10 completed 32 full games, eight seed pairs per difficulty, on a separate Accelerator queue. When the SFT used its previous sampled movement, direction changes fell from 59.94% to 36.39% of decisions on Hard and from 59.85% to 35.75% on Lunatic. Mean Stage 1 progress on Hard changed by +339 ticks (paired 95% interval −42 to +722) and on Lunatic by +171 (+52 to +317). Both policies reached Stage 2 in 0/8 credits per difficulty and exhausted all spare lives. Mean remaining Power changed from 0.106 to 0.063 on Hard and 0.319 to 0.250 on Lunatic. These are eight-pair, within-title effects on game progress. TH17 has a separate original-game cohort. On TH17, conditioning on the previous sampled movement increased the fraction of repeated directions from 36.8% to 62.5% on Hard and from 34.7% to 61.3% on Lunatic. Hard progress changed by -40 native ticks (paired 95% interval -466 to +386). Lunatic progress changed by +701 ticks (-130 to +1,532). Neither controller reached Stage 2 in any of the 32 TH17 credits. Across TH10 and TH17, these were 64 ordinary stock starts.
