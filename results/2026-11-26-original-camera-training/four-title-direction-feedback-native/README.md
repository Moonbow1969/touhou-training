# From replay direction prediction to original-game survival

TH10 completed 32 full games, eight seed pairs per difficulty, on a separate Accelerator queue. When the SFT used its previous sampled movement, direction changes fell from 59.94% to 36.39% of decisions on Hard and from 59.85% to 35.75% on Lunatic. Mean Stage 1 progress on Hard changed by +339 ticks (paired 95% interval −42 to +722) and on Lunatic by +171 (+52 to +317). Both policies reached Stage 2 in 0/8 credits per difficulty and exhausted all spare lives. Mean remaining Power changed from 0.106 to 0.063 on Hard and 0.319 to 0.250 on Lunatic. These are eight-pair, within-title effects on game progress. TH17 has a separate original-game cohort.
