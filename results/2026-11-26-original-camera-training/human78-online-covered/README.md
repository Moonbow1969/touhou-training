# Three-game PPO from the expanded human SFT

The original-game test completed 144 full credits across TH10, TH12 and TH13. In every title and difficulty, SFT and PPO each reached Stage 2 in 0/12 credits and finished with zero spare lives. On TH12 Hard the two policies averaged 2,524 and 2,840 native ticks to final death and 1.17 and 0.67 remaining Bombs. On Lunatic the corresponding values were 2,249 and 2,104 ticks and 0.67 and 1.25 Bombs. This comparison used 48 TH12 credits with continuous control and 26,707 real decisions. The spatial-attention SFT trained on 235,457 human camera decisions from TH10, TH12 and TH13. Its deployed actor collected 48 original Hard and Lunatic training games, eight per title and difficulty. The new PPO completed four epochs over 22,857 playable decisions. The parent controls movement, focus, shot and cast directly from the camera.
