**Which comparison does this support?** Across shots there are now three training identities and one held identity. Reimu A alone still has one training identity, Boredperson, and one previously evaluated held identity, zylo. The new players each use different shot types. Combining shots confounds player and shot, and adjacent frames are strongly dependent. We trained no new weights. The previously reported six native credit pairs for the locator intervention therefore retain their original interpretation.

**Source search.** We inspected headers of 3,088 unique public replay URLs and 3,409 local replay paths. The new source files came from the Gensokyo archive. The three original submissions are tagged as Normal runs, and all three uploaders' comments identify their respective trial runs. Archive submitter names, decoded internal player names, exact native rank, shot, stage lengths and source SHA are retained. Three further 0.11 inputs were Easy replays from the same author and were excluded. A historical Hard-only event links its original leaderboard and fourteen replay ZIP names. The current site returned HTTP 403 for those downloads and the queried archive lookup returned no snapshots. Its players have no place in the Normal author counts. [New sources and split](shot-tagged-registration.json) · [Submitter metadata](archive-author-provenance.json) · [Source checks](source-index.zip)

**Native validation.** At each of 26,446 retained captures the previous and current native input words exactly matched their original decoded replay. Every one of 483 full-color original samples regenerated the policy image byte for byte. Thirty-one observations had inconsistent center/rectangle geometry, and all supervised windows touching these rows as either of their two observation frames were excluded. Four-head labels were independently replay-decoded on Windows, including native Bomb rising edges. Head definitions are documented in the collection tools. The table's sprite metric is a separate full-field image check. Boredperson Stage 2's training scene includes the source template frames. Zylo uses Boredperson's unchanged template bank. Each additional shot uses its Stage 1 bank and tests on later Stage 1 windows and Stage 2. [Stage counts, movement histograms, sources and hashes](index.json)

**Storage.** The eight immutable stage ZIPs total 445,572,924 bytes. Each GitHub release digest matched the locally computed SHA-256 and size. We downloaded Pykrete's published Reimu B Stage 1 again to verify the complete archive with an independent offline checker and the original public replay. The release files contain complete sampled policy pixels, sparse original full-color frames, player coordinates, original keys, original start/exit screenshots and per-file digests. The measured two-host peak bound, including rejected engineering attempts and transient copies, stayed below 627 MB against a 1 GB budget. [Deployed sensor digests](deployed-observer-sha256.txt)

**Dataset totals.** The previously released Reimu A Stage 1 captures contribute another 5,279 causal pairs. Together, the two releases contain 25,013 TRAIN windows and 6,283 held windows. Held data come from one player. Each split was fixed by player identity before any new stage was inspected.
