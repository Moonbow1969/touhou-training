# Human-camera policy after adding twelve retail TH17 players

The shared camera SFT used Hard and Lunatic examples from four titles. The training set has 47 synchronized stages and 148,415 distinct usable decisions. The eight game-by-difficulty buckets supplied 192,000 presentations in 3,000 updates of 64. The TH17 collection attempted 24 preselected new stages from 12 players and admitted 22 after checking score and resource synchronization against the retail executable. These contributed 78,273 decisions, with 107 captured Bomb onsets. [Training log and source hashes](report.json) · [TH17 synchronization audit](../../human-retail17-wave2/train-original-synchronized-frozen.json) · [Raw retail replay and camera release](../../human-retail17-wave2/train-github-release.json)

On two previously used TH17 development players, correct directions increased from 2,239 to 2,478 out of 5,024. Correct direction changes increased from 425 to 462 out of 1,925. Correct starts from rest dropped from 64 to 42 out of 642. One player accounted for 238 of the 239 additional correct directions. Neither player pressed Bomb.
