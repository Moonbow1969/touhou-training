# How much Bomb stock remained immediately before a native life loss?

Two consecutive behavior policies each supplied 16 Hard and 16 Lunatic full credits on TH12 and TH17. We read Bomb stock at the immediately preceding observed native sample for each of 384 life losses. For TH17 we also read positive changes in each fragment counter at constant life stock within a stage.

| Behavior | Title | Difficulty | Life losses with a Bomb available | Same-life life-piece increases | Same-life Bomb-piece increases |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Five-title generation 2 | TH12 | Hard | 33/48 | unmeasured | unmeasured |
| Five-title generation 2 | TH12 | Lunatic | 33/48 | unmeasured | unmeasured |
| Five-title generation 2 | TH17 | Hard | 31/48 | 0 | 1 |
| Five-title generation 2 | TH17 | Lunatic | 36/48 | 0 | 0 |
| Six-title generation 1 | TH12 | Hard | 27/48 | unmeasured | unmeasured |
| Six-title generation 1 | TH12 | Lunatic | 26/48 | unmeasured | unmeasured |
| Six-title generation 1 | TH17 | Hard | 21/48 | 1 | 2 |
| Six-title generation 1 | TH17 | Lunatic | 25/48 | 0 | 0 |

A Bomb remained before 133/192 losses under the first behavior and 99/192 under the second. These samples describe when the player lost stock. They cannot establish that an earlier Bomb would have saved a life. Across 64 TH17 credits, one life-piece increase and three Bomb-piece increases met the observation criteria. The small number limits the learning signal available from these events in this Stage 1 population. TH12 fragment counters were outside this observer.
