# Claude Opus 5.5 architecture and reward review on November 27, 2026

The review used original TH08 endpoint data, the matching replay experiment and the TH07 replay and PPO results. TH08's two fresh SFT/PPO pairs both ended in Stage 1 with three Bombs. Its 60,638 action windows include just 13 distinct training Bomb onsets, so movement and Bomb support have different effective sample sizes. The optimizer's four original training credits included four Bomb actions, while all 12 matched SFT context and SFT/PPO test credits included zero. Claude prioritized an offline architecture comparison before collecting a new reward cohort. TH06's registered acquisition can continue in parallel.

The first four-arm experiment uses the same original 58,696 causal windows, four ordered image states, location auxiliary loss, batch draws and 500 updates per arm, repeated at three declared seeds. All four policies train their candidate weights from scratch for the matched architecture comparison. The existing Normal current-state SFT provides common Focus targets. Candidates do not read native coordinates during inference. The normal 7×6 pooled control has 233,804 parameters. The 14×12 spatial readout and keypoints have 234,566. The widened pooled readout with comparable multiplication count has 520,608. The wide pooled readout has 922,936. The spatial arm uses 368.0 million estimated multiply-accumulates per four-state input. The comparable pooled arm uses 355.7 million, a 3.4% difference. The wide arm is a separate capacity test with approximately 3.95 times the baseline parameters.

“Busy” means the top quarter of local image-change energy within each held stage. The offline promotion requirements for every one of the three training seeds are at least 1.5 percentage points improvement on busy frames, gains on at least five of six held stages, and overall direction NLL ≤ baseline + 0.01. Spatial must additionally reach at most 20 original playfield pixels median error on an independent linear location readout. Two previously used held pilots provide the six development stages. The registered original-game gate calls for a verified four-native-tick decision interval and 12 crossed-order original credits per arm. The return metric is the Stage 1 life-loss rate per native tick. A rate ratio at or below 0.65 calls for a new 30-credit check. A ratio at or above 0.90 ends this architecture experiment. H100 comes back into consideration if increasing capacity shows a consistent benefit and learner time becomes the bottleneck.

Claude proposed using observed normalized original progress plus a verified stage clear and testing the effect of a spare-life potential. Bomb expenditure and pickups have zero weight in this particular ablation. Both arms would use the same learned Bomb initialization if exploration changes are needed. The implemented reward deliberately reconciles γ and the critic bootstrap: a real absorbing terminal has zero potential, and a continuing Stage 2 state keeps its potential. Existing training credits lack a verified per-action stage clock. Fresh original traces must provide that evidence before training the reward comparison.
