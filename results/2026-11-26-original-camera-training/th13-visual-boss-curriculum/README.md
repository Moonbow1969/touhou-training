# TH13 Easy PPO from a live final-spell handoff

Sixteen registered acquisitions started with ordinary stock. A guide brought the game to the last spell, where all guide keys were released and the frozen human SFT took over. Eleven starts qualified, while five never reached the handoff criteria. Three of the 11 policy suffixes entered Stage 2 and eight ended on Stage 1. All 3,342 verified policy actions from all 11 qualified suffixes entered the PPO update. The optimizer excluded every guide action. The four PPO epochs were accepted and maximum final title KL was 0.00368.
