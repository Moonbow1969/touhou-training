# TH12 resource pickups and respawn refills

Across 92 complete training credits in the original TH12 trial, the native HUD recorded zero increases in spare lives and 107 increases in full Bomb stock. Every Bomb increase ended at two and occurred within 0.809 seconds of a confirmed lost spare. The audit excludes those 107 transitions from pickup labels. A positive reward for each Bomb-stock increase would reward respawns in this sample. The source receipts require three consecutive active observations of a changed stock. Each event in the ledger retains its source trajectory checksum. [Training event ledger](training-resource-events.json)

Five independent original color-frame pairs show a spare disappearing and the Bomb reserve returning to two. The actor schema records full stocks. Collecting a fragment can leave both full stocks unchanged, and the schema has no UFO type or collection event. Attributing a pickup reward to any of these actions would require an additional labeled signal. [Native HUD before and after respawn](respawn-stock-native-hud.jpg)

**Clock alignment.** These runs timestamped the periodic color capture with Linux `/proc/uptime`, which includes time spent suspended. The action and reward stream used `CLOCK_MONOTONIC`. On Steam Deck the difference was 2.908339 seconds. Subtracting it reduced the median absolute image-match residual from 2.8863 to 0.0331 seconds across 44 independent color observations. Entropy's corresponding offset was zero, with a 0.0315-second median residual across 22 color observations. The correction admits a final visible midboss countdown at 16.31 in portable PPO3 held seed 761001, bringing that exploratory count to 6/12. Input frames, physical key checks, rewards, stock trajectories, native terminal screenshots, Stage 2 outcomes, and credit durations already use the actor clock. New collector builds timestamp both streams with that clock. [Calibration and original-frame verification](color-clock-calibration.json)
