# Adding TH08 to original-game PPO

The first TH08 learner restored the actor and critic from the second joint TH10, TH11, TH12, TH13 and TH17 PPO generation. It ran four more PPO epochs using four complete TH08 Hard starts (2,672 policy decisions) and four Lunatic starts (1,684). All four epochs passed the fixed KL gate. Final joint KL was 0.00341 on Hard and 0.00352 on Lunatic. Replaying original frames and four-head actions reproduced logged probabilities within 0.000006 in log space. These 4,356 decisions establish a real TH08 online update. Its independent paired evaluation is registered for eight seeds per difficulty. Each rollout uses the original trial's rank glyph, life and Bomb stars and first continue prompt. Only policy actions preceding the first verified stock exhaustion enter PPO. Each spare life lost costs 1, including the final life. A measured Bomb spend costs 0.03 per unit, and replenishment on respawn is excluded. Component returns and native evidence are available for every credit. [Selected original trajectories and component returns](selected-training/) · [Training registration](protocol.json)
