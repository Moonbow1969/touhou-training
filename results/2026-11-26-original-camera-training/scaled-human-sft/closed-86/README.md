# Expanding the human vision training set on one RTX 5090

The expanded set contains 290,583 four-tick decisions from 110 original TH10, TH12 and TH13 replay stages with 97 credited training authors. The earlier set contains 235,457 decisions from 88 stages with 77 credited authors. Of the 96 registered additional capture sources, 86 passed qualification and ten were excluded. Each split keeps the six held people separate from training. [Source denominators and exclusions](registered-source-eligibility.json) · [Training split](protocol.json)

At 44,149 updates the expanded actor received 2,825,536 sampled presentations and saw 286,316 unique training decisions. The same optimizer stream continued to 54,485 updates and 3,487,040 presentations. Across 28,241 held human decisions, movement accuracy was 65.47% and 65.41% at the respective checkpoints. On 7,681 actual direction changes it was 22.76% and 23.83%. The top 5% of estimated Bomb-onset risks captured two and three of the 32 recorded onsets. The top 5% of Bomb risks caught 3/32 versus 2/32 actual onsets.
